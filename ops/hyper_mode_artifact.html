Foundation

The Three Laws

1
The Burst Law
"Work happens in 3-10 day bursts separated by ~7 day gaps."
Energy is finite. Ship in concentrated bursts, then rest. Don't fight the rhythm.
Burst 1 (5d, 79c)
Burst 2 (10d, 428c)
Burst 3 (8d, 41c)
2
The Deletion Law
"40% of Hyperbrowser was deleted to ship the real thing."
Mature products delete as much as they build. If you're not deleting, you're accumulating debt.
EARLY PHASES (0-3)
90% build
10%
MIDDLE PHASES (4-6)
60% build
40% delete
LATE PHASES (7-8)
50% build
50% delete
3
The Atomic Unit Law
"Every product has ONE core primitive."
Find it. Everything else orbits it. If a feature doesn't serve the atomic unit, kill it.

Get Started

Quick Start

1

Drop HYPER.yaml in your repo root

Copy the template above. Fill in your product name, ICP, and positioning. Leave atomic_unit: null until Phase 6.

2

Load the hyper-mode skill in your AI coding session

Add hyper-mode to your skills list. It reads HYPER.yaml and guides your session based on phase and burst state.

3

Ship your Spark (Phase 0)

Build the dumbest possible version that works. One demo. No auth, no deploy, no CI. Just prove the idea breathes.

4

Ride the burst rhythm

Ship hard for 3-10 days, then rest. The protocol tracks your state and adjusts. No guilt for gaps.

5

Let the phases guide you

Don't skip ahead. The benchmarks show where Hyperbrowser was at each phase. Use them as a compass, not a clock.
